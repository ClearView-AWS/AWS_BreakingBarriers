## Say (word-for-word)

Parents don’t lose good schools because the ratings are wrong.

They lose them because the one fact they need — after-school care, a gifted program, a bus — isn’t on the card. And calling every office during work hours is a second job.

GreatSchools already reaches on the order of forty million families a year, across a hundred and fifty thousand school profiles. The data gap is not rare.

In our own eval, sixty-eight percent of need-bearing searches show at least one Unknown in the top eight results. On the real GreatSchools sample, after-school care is missing on every single school — twenty-five of twenty-five.

So we built Guided Search differently.

We investigate. You decide.

Missing facts stay on the screen. And when enough parents ask the same question, we go get the answer — once — for everyone.

Live demo is up. Over to our driver.
